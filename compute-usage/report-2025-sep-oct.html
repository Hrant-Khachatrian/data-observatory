Notable Workloads

The most GPU-intensive projects at YSU ML Group (1,000+ GPU hours):

  • In-Context Learning — fine-tunes 4B-parameter Gemma models.
  • Data Selection — pre-trains DINO-style ViT-B models on millions of images.
  • 3D Molecules — fine-tunes Llama/Qwen models up to 1B parameters.
  • Satellite Foundation Models — adapts multiple ViT-B models across dozens of tasks.
  • Visual Language Navigation — fine-tunes Qwen2.5-VL-3B and Isaac 0.1 (2B).
  • Molecular Optimization — inference and GRPO on 380M–1B-parameter LLMs.

Computational Material Science Lab

Focus for Improvement

  • Improve disk monitoring: weekend troughs often trace back to disk saturation; earlier alerting would protect throughput.
  • Promote CPU backfill: advertise surplus CPU capacity to CPU-only projects to push toward the 80% utilization target.
