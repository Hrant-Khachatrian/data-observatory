NISAR S-band coverage

Released GCOV footprints extend beyond India. This catalog snapshot shows where products exist, rather than where the mission is designed to observe.

Catalog queried October 10, 2026 · Acquisition dates July 8–October 9, 2026 · Metadata only

13,590 GCOV products represent 13,586 distinct acquisition frames. Their footprints intersect 85 country or territory boundaries in the global basemap. Coverage includes India, selected sites in Europe, the Americas, Africa, Asia and Australia, and extensive polar observations. No GCOV footprint intersects Armenia in this snapshot.

What is available?

ISRO's July 24, 2026 release notice explicitly describes S-band collections over India and global science locations. Operational products are released from Cycle 25, which began July 8, 2026. Older observations are scheduled for phased reprocessing. This is evidence of selected worldwide coverage, not continuous global coverage or a guarantee of a usable observation at every location.

GCOV is the Level-2 geocoded polarimetric covariance product. Its diagonal terms describe radar backscatter intensity; available cross terms depend on the polarization mode. It is a practical starting point for cross-sensor land-cover experiments. Other collections describe different representations of observations or observation pairs:

ProductsWhat they represent
RSLC / GSLCSingle-acquisition complex radar data containing amplitude and phase, in radar coordinates / geocoded coordinates.
GCOVSingle-acquisition geocoded covariance and terrain-corrected backscatter. This report inventories GCOV only.
RIFG / RUNW / GUNWInterferometric products from pairs of acquisitions: wrapped phase, unwrapped phase in radar coordinates, and geocoded unwrapped phase.
ROFF / GOFFOffsets estimated between acquisitions, in radar coordinates / geocoded coordinates.

Implications for the cross-SAR experiment

European S-band footprints make cross-sensor studies with European land-cover labels worth checking. Country intersection alone does not establish overlap with labeled patches. Such studies need intersections of actual label polygons with S-band and L-band footprints, explicit acquisition-date differences, and comparable spatial support.

Armenia currently cannot support a three-way Sentinel-1 / NISAR L / NISAR S comparison using this released S-band GCOV snapshot. A corresponding L-band inventory is needed to quantify overlap elsewhere. Neither coverage nor matching labels guarantees comparable backscatter: polarization, resolution, incidence angle, season and processing need to be recorded.

Method and limits

The authenticated Bhoonidhi catalog was queried across the mission period, starting July 1, 2025. Because ordinary pagination failed, date intervals were recursively divided until each completed query returned fewer than the 500-record cap. Overlapping date boundaries were deduplicated by product ID. Completed query windows are preserved below.

Products were grouped by OBSID, Track, Frame, RCID, Polarization for acquisition-frame counts; this removes four repeated product representations. Footprints use the catalog's ImgLatLon scene corners, with antimeridian crossings normalized. Country counts use intersections with Natural Earth 110m boundaries. Frames spanning borders count in multiple countries, and the simplified map is an overview, not a pixel-validity mask. Counts reflect one changing catalog snapshot; they do not include unreleased observations or claim completeness across other product types.

The CSV preserves the input along-track and across-track resolution fields. These describe catalog metadata and should not be equated with GCOV output pixel spacing or a single effective image resolution. Inspect product grid metadata before choosing the experiment's common resolution. ISRO also reports residual banding and radio-frequency interference in the current release.
